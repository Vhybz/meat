MI~CORAZON FRESHMEAT BUTCHERY - SYSTEM DOCUMENTATION
USER ROLE: CASHIER (RETAIL & CUSTOMER SERVICE)

1. YOUR ROLE IN THE SYSTEM
The Cashier is the face of Mi~Corazon and the final gatekeeper of the company's revenue. You are responsible for receiving stock from the butcher, processing sales accurately, and managing customer loyalty and debts.

2. CORE WORKFLOWS
- Incoming Stock Verification: When meat arrives from the butcher house, scan the barcode attached to the package. This "unlocks" the stock and makes it available for sale on your POS screen.
- Point of Sale (POS): Process customer orders efficiently. You can toggle between Retail and Wholesale prices depending on the customer's purchase volume.
- Customer Management: Register every regular customer. Use the "Favorites" feature to automatically apply promotions to our most loyal clients.
- Debt Tracking: If a customer has a balance, you MUST select their profile in the system before finalizing the sale. Record every partial payment accurately in the "Debt Tracker."
- Sale Corrections: If you make a mistake in a transaction, use the "Request Correction" feature to notify the Admin. Explain the mistake clearly.
- AI Support: Tap the Sparkles icon to ask the AI about shop policies, customer service tips, or how to handle complex payments.

3. ETHICAL USAGE GUIDELINES
- Financial Honesty: Ensure every payment (Cash, MoMo, or Bank Deposit) is recorded for its exact amount. Never bypass the system for a sale.
- Stock Integrity: Do not sell items that have not been verified through the "Incoming Stock" scanner. This preserves the accuracy of our inventory logs.
- Privacy: Maintain customer confidentiality. Phone numbers and purchase histories should never be shared outside of business requirements.
- Transparency: Always offer the customer a receipt (digital or SMS) so they can verify their purchase and discounts.

4. TIPS FOR SUCCESS
- Check the "System Alerts" on your dashboard regularly for low-stock warnings or incoming transfers.
- In case of network failure, the POS will work in Offline Mode. Keep processing sales; they will sync with the Cloud as soon as you are back online.
