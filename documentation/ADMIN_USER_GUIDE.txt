MI~CORAZON FRESHMEAT BUTCHERY - SYSTEM DOCUMENTATION
USER ROLE: ADMINISTRATOR (OWNER/MANAGER)

1. YOUR ROLE IN THE SYSTEM
The Administrator is the overseer of the entire business ecosystem. You are responsible for the financial health, staff accountability, and strategic growth of Mi~Corazon. Your portal provides high-level analytics and control over all workstations.

2. CORE WORKFLOWS
- Staff Management: Approve new account registrations, assign primary and secondary roles, and manage temporary promotions (e.g., promoting a cashier to a manager for a week).
- Inventory Control: Set the retail, wholesale, and cost prices for all products. You must monitor stock levels and define low-stock thresholds.
- Financial Oversight: Review the Dashboard for real-time Gross and Net Profit. Monitor the "Business Expenses" ledger to ensure operational costs are controlled.
- Procurement Analytics: Use "Butcher Analytics" to compare animal purchase costs (Farm Price) against their market value. This helps you identify the best suppliers and buying strategies.
- Debt Management: Monitor the "Debt Tracker" to ensure customer balances are being collected and verified.
- AI Assistance: Use the Sparkles icon on your dashboard to ask the AI Assistant for business advice, profit analysis, or system help.

3. ETHICAL USAGE GUIDELINES
- Data Integrity: Never manipulate sales or expense records to misrepresent the business's financial status.
- Fairness: Ensure staff roles and salary configurations are managed transparently and based on merit or contract agreements.
- Privacy: Protect customer contact information found in the Customer Directory. Do not use this data for non-business purposes.
- Accountability: Use the "Audit Logs" to verify actions fairly. When a staff member reports a mistake (e.g., a Sale Correction request), investigate with empathy before rectifying.

4. SYSTEM MAINTENANCE
- Regularly review the "System Settings" to ensure branch locations and contact details are up to date.
- Perform monthly exports of "Daily Sales Reports" and "Expense Ledgers" for tax compliance (GRA).
